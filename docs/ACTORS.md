# 2.3.2. Description of Actors

| #   | Actor Name | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Admin      | Organization owner or administrator who manages the entire chat assistant system. They have full permissions to configure widget settings, manage team members, upload knowledge documents, manage plugins and integrations, view all conversations, update subscription plans, and monitor system usage. |
| 2   | TeamMember | Staff member or support agent within an organization who handles customer conversations. They can view and respond to customer chats, monitor conversation status, and provide human support when AI escalates issues. They have limited access compared to Admin.                                        |
| 3   | Customer   | End users who interact with the AI assistant through the embedded chat widget. They can start conversations, send messages, view their conversation history, and receive responses from the AI agent or human support staff. Authentication is handled through temporary contact sessions.                |
